      Crater Patrol

      Your rover has to cross four sectors of the Moon. Every crater field is sealed by an unbalanced equation. Put a coefficient in the crater in front of each formula. When the atoms balance, the craters fill and you roll on.

      • Change only the coefficients. Subscripts are part of the formula; changing one makes a different substance.
      • Use the lowest whole numbers. Answers that are all doubled overfill the craters.
      • A wrong fill costs a shield. Lose all four and you restart that sector with new equations.
      • Mission control can help. A hint costs 10 points; a repair drone locks in one coefficient for 30.

      ← → pick a crater · 0–9 type a number · ↑ ↓ adjust · Enter fill · H hint. On a phone or tablet, tap a crater and use the keypad. Sound and music start when you launch; switch them off at the top right.

        River Run

        ← → or A D steer · Space fire

        Your jet has reached Titan, Saturn's largest moon. Its rivers and seas are liquid methane, but the air has no free oxygen, so the engine carries its own and burns CH₄ + 2 O₂ → CO₂ + 2 H₂O.

        • Both tanks read in moles. Fly over a depot and convert what it holds into moles to refuel. A wrong answer spills half.
        • Watch the limiting reactant. The engine uses O₂ twice as fast as CH₄. Whichever tank runs dry first stops the engine.
        • Bridges are locked. Solve the stoichiometry problem to fire a missile through. A wrong answer costs a shield.
        • Kraken Mare waits at the end. Its three tentacles are a limiting reactant, theoretical yield and percent yield problem.

        Keep a calculator handy. Answers within 5% count. ← → steer, Space fires; on a phone, use the buttons under the screen or drag on it. Crashing into banks or enemies costs fuel, not shields.

          Retro Reactions · Stage 3 of 4

          Bond Defender

          Electron thieves are raiding the molecules on Europa, Jupiter's icy moon. For each molecule, count its valence electrons, shoot down the thieves to get the electron pairs back, then rebuild the Lewis structure with exactly those pairs.

          • Count first. Add the valence electrons of every atom, then adjust for the charge.
          • Every pair goes somewhere. Click a connection to make it a bond, or an atom to give it a lone pair.
          • Octets matter. A structure that breaks the octet rule, or a wrong count, costs a shield.
          • Thieves that escape become radicals and chase you. Shoot them to get the pairs back.

          ← → fly · ↑ ↓ climb and dive · Space fire. Crashing costs points, not shields.

            Back on Earth, Lord Barkium and the Reaction Gang have taken over the city. Beat them one at a time in a chemistry showdown.

            • Five gang members, five topics. Balancing, atoms and molar mass, moles, Lewis structures and mole ratios.
            • Answer to attack. A right answer lands a combo. Two combos knock a gang member out.
            • A wrong answer costs a shield. You get 3 per fight, and you always see why the right answer is right.
            • Lord Barkium (Ba) is last. He takes three hits: one balancing, one mole question, one Lewis structure.
