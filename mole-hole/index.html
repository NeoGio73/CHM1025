A chemistry vocabulary arcade. Read the clue, then dig your way to the crystal with the matching term.

CHAPTERS

Dig arrows / WASD   Throw catalyst space   Pause P   Mute M
Four crystals are buried in the dirt, each with a term. Tunnel to the one that matches the clue. Grime blobs chase you through your tunnels. Dig under a boulder to drop it on them, or throw your catalyst, which bounces through the tunnels and comes back after a few seconds. Collect electrons for points.

SOUND TEST

Original game, art, music, and sounds. Inspired by early-1980s digging games. Clues are written for this game and are not quoted from any textbook.
